How to read this

Every figure is Hyros' own: cost, clicks, impressions, leads, sales and revenue (one-time plus recurring) credited by last click to the Fall Summit 2026 Meta ad sets. No lead tag is applied, so leads and sales include anyone Hyros credits to these ad sets, repeat registrants included. Hyros cost can trail Meta's own spend by a few hours, and Hyros caches a repeated query for about four minutes. Revenue is last-click, so a sale whose closing click was an email or organic visit is not credited to the ad that first brought the buyer in; that is why it reads lower than a tag-based count. MOF campaigns are kept out of every total above their own section. Campaigns with no activity are left out. Ads and creatives are deliberately not shown.
